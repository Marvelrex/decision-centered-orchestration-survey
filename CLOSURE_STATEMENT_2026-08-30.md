# Screening closure statement (2026-08-30)

The author closed the screening process on 2026-08-30 with the analytical corpus frozen at 84 report keys (40 core interventions).

Disposition of the records that were still open at closure:

- 90 records marked "uncertain" at the title-and-abstract stage were not pursued to full text. They remain excluded from the corpus. AI-generated suggestions for these records (including a publication-venue quality grading) are preserved in `04_screening_ledger/` for transparency, but no inclusion decision was derived from them.
- 8 remaining new-core candidates that had passed the automated title-and-abstract screen were not assessed at full text and remain excluded.
- During the final review round the author did assess a set of candidates at full text with source-verified evidence extraction. Three reports were included as core interventions on 2026-08-30 (PAX, GreenKube, and the Kubernetes microgrid scheduler). Five candidates were rejected on publication-venue quality and one on scope (video transcoding rather than container orchestration). These decisions are logged in `04_screening_ledger/AUTHOR_DECISIONS_2026-08-30.md` and in the venue-graded candidate list.

All decisions were made by the single human reviewer. The corpus, the manuscript counts, the PRISMA figure, and the reconstruction artifacts in this package are mutually consistent as of this date.
